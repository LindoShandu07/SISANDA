To my Sisanda Mchunu,
aka iMama, aka my baby, aka Nana ❤️🥹❤️

My baby, my love, my Nana, my iMama. ❤️❤️

I honestly don't even know where to begin because sometimes words are just not enough to explain how much someone means to you. Sometimes you can sit down and try to find the perfect words to express everything in your heart, but no matter how much you write, you still feel like there is so much more you want to say. That's exactly how I feel right now. I want you to understand how much I appreciate you, how much I value you, and how grateful I am to have you in my life. I want you to know that none of your efforts go unnoticed, none of your patience is taken for granted, and none of the love you have shown me is something I will ever consider small or ordinary.

Ssanda Mchunu, my iMama, my baby, my Nana, I need you to know that I appreciate you more than you could ever imagine. I appreciate the person you are, the heart you have, the love you give, and even the things you do that you probably think I don't notice. I appreciate the way you try, the way you make an effort, the way you continue to show up, and the way you have been patient with me even when things have not always been easy. I know that being patient with another person is not always simple. Loving someone doesn't mean that every day will be perfect, that every conversation will be easy, or that everything will always go according to plan. Sometimes it takes understanding, sacrifice, forgiveness, communication, and a lot of patience to keep choosing each other. And for all the times you have done that, I want to say thank you, my love. Thank you from the bottom of my heart. ❤️

Thank you for being patient with me, Nana. Thank you for the times you have tried to understand me, even when I have struggled to explain myself properly. Thank you for the moments when you could have given up on trying to understand where I was coming from, but instead, you gave me room to express myself. Thank you for every conversation, every effort, every moment of reassurance, and every little thing you have done to make things better between us. I may not always have the perfect words to say it, and I may not always express my appreciation in the way you deserve, but please believe me when I say that I see you. I see your efforts, I see your heart, and I see the person behind everything you do.

My baby, I know that I am not a perfect person. I know there are things about me that I still need to work on, things I am still learning, and moments when I could handle situations better than I do. I know that sometimes I might not get everything right, and there may be times when understanding me requires more patience than it should. But one thing I never want you to question is how much I appreciate you. I never want you to feel like all the love and effort you pour into me is going to waste. I never want you to feel as though you are giving your best to someone who doesn't recognise what he has in front of him. Because, Nana, I do recognise it. I recognise you, and I recognise the blessing it is to have someone like you in my life.

I don't want to become so comfortable with having you around that I forget how special you are. I don't want us to get so caught up in the everyday things that I stop appreciating the little things that make you who you are. I don't want to become someone who only realises the value of your efforts when you stop making them. You deserve to be appreciated while you are here, while you are trying, while you are loving, and while you are giving your heart to the people who matter to you. And I want to do my part in making sure you know that you matter to me, my love. You matter more than you sometimes realise.

Ssanda, one thing I want you to understand is that I will always appreciate you, no matter what happens in life. No matter what season we find ourselves in, no matter what challenges we may face, and no matter how much life changes around us, I will never look back at the effort you have made and consider it meaningless. I will never pretend that your patience meant nothing. I will never forget the kindness you have shown me or the moments when you chose to put in the effort to understand me. The fact that someone is willing to give you their time, their care, their attention, and their patience is something that should never be taken lightly. Those things are precious, and I want you to know that I understand their value.

My Nana, I appreciate you on the good days, when everything feels easy and we can laugh, smile, joke around, and enjoy each other's presence without having to think too much about anything. But I also appreciate you on the difficult days, when we might not see everything the same way, when we have to talk things through, and when we need to make an extra effort to understand one another. I appreciate you when you are happy, when you are tired, when you need reassurance, when you need someone to listen, and when you simply need to be loved without having to explain why. I want you to know that you don't have to be perfect for me to appreciate you. You don't have to get everything right all the time. You are human, just like me, and you deserve the same patience, understanding, and kindness that you have tried to give.

I hope that, as time goes on, I can become better at showing you how much I value you. I hope my actions will speak just as loudly as my words. I hope I can become more intentional about appreciating you, not only when I feel emotional or when I sit down to write you a letter like this, but also in the ordinary moments that make up our lives. I want to learn how to love you in ways that make you feel seen, heard, respected, valued, and safe. I want to be more mindful of your feelings, more willing to listen, and more understanding of the things that matter to you. I want to make sure that appreciation is not just something I tell you about, but something you can actually experience through the way I treat you.

Because, baby, you deserve that. You deserve someone who notices when you are trying. You deserve someone who doesn't make you feel as though you have to beg for recognition. You deserve someone who can acknowledge when you have made an effort, even if the outcome isn't perfect. You deserve someone who can say, "I see what you are doing, Nana, and I appreciate you for it." And I want to be that person for you. I want to be someone who recognises your efforts without you always having to point them out. I want to be someone who understands that sometimes the smallest gestures carry the biggest meanings.

My iMama, thank you for being yourself. Thank you for bringing your own personality, your own heart, your own way of loving, and your own beautiful presence into my life. Thank you for the smiles, the laughs, the conversations, and the memories we have made. Thank you for the times you have made me feel cared for and for the moments when your presence alone has meant something to me. Thank you for giving me opportunities to learn, to grow, and to understand more about what it means to value another person. There are things we learn about ourselves through the people we meet, and I am grateful for the lessons, experiences, and moments that having you in my life has brought me.

And Nana, please don't ever think that because I might not always know how to express myself perfectly, I don't feel these things deeply. Sometimes I may struggle to put my feelings into words, but that doesn't mean there is nothing in my heart. Sometimes I need to slow down and really think about the person standing beside me, the things they do, and the love they show. When I do that, I realise just how much there is about you that I should be grateful for. I realise that behind all the little moments are two people trying to understand each other, trying to grow, and trying to make something meaningful out of the time they share.

I also want you to know that I appreciate the patience you have shown me, but I don't want you to feel that you must always be the patient one. Your feelings matter too, my love. Your happiness matters. Your peace matters. Your needs matter. You are allowed to have difficult days, to express your frustrations, to ask for understanding, and to need reassurance yourself. You don't always have to be the strong one or the one who understands everything. I want there to be room for you to be human with me, just as I am human with you. I want us to be able to learn from each other, support each other, and make room for both of our feelings. I appreciate everything you have given me, but I also want to keep learning how to give you the kind of care and consideration you deserve.

My baby, I cannot promise you that I will never make a mistake. I cannot promise that life will always be easy or that we will never have moments when we need to work through something. But I can promise that I will never consider your efforts worthless simply because we are facing a difficult moment. I can promise that I want to keep learning, keep growing, and keep becoming more aware of the ways I can treat you better. I want to be able to look at myself honestly, recognise where I need to improve, and take responsibility for the things that are mine to work on. I don't want you to have to keep repeating yourself for me to understand that something matters to you. I want to listen, learn, and put effort into becoming better.

And when I say I will always appreciate you, I mean it regardless of what life brings. I mean that I will always be grateful for the time you have given me, the patience you have shown me, and the effort you have put into the connection we share. I mean that I will not reduce your worth to one disagreement, one misunderstanding, or one difficult day. I mean that the good you have brought into my life deserves to be recognised and respected. You are more than the moments when things are difficult. You are more than any disagreement we may have. You are a whole person with a heart, dreams, feelings, hopes, and your own battles, and I appreciate you for all of that.

Ssanda Mchunu, my iMama, my baby, my Nana, if there is one thing I want you to take away from this letter, it is this: you are appreciated, you are valued, and your efforts mean something to me. I hope you never have to question whether I recognise the patience you have shown me. I hope you never feel that your kindness has gone unnoticed. And I hope that even on days when I don't express myself as well as I should, you can remember that I am genuinely grateful for the person you are and the place you hold in my life.

Thank you for being part of my journey. Thank you for every little effort, every moment of patience, every conversation, and every opportunity we have had to understand each other better. Thank you for the things you have done that I may not even have fully acknowledged at the time. I may not be able to repay every act of kindness with something equal, but I can make a conscious effort to recognise it, cherish it, and never treat it as something I am automatically entitled to receive.

I hope life gives you so many reasons to smile. I hope you continue to grow into the person you want to become. I hope you achieve the things you dream about, find peace in the things that matter to you, and always remember how deserving you are of genuine love and appreciation. I hope you know that you don't have to earn your worth through what you do for others. You are worthy of appreciation simply because you are you. Your heart, your individuality, your presence, and your humanity are enough.

And finally, my Nana, my baby, my iMama, thank you. Thank you for being you. Thank you for your patience. Thank you for your efforts. Thank you for your understanding. Thank you for the love you have shown me in your own way. Thank you for every moment you have chosen to try, even when trying hasn't always been easy. I hope I can continue to show you that none of it is wasted and that I am not blind to the beautiful things you bring into my life.

No matter what tomorrow brings, I will always be grateful that I got to know you, share moments with you, learn from you, and appreciate the person you are. I will always have respect for the efforts you have made and the patience you have shown me. And I will always wish for you to feel loved, appreciated, understood, and valued, because you deserve to experience those things just as much as anyone else.

I love you, my Ssanda Mchunu. I love you, iMama. I love you, my baby. I love you, Nana. ❤️🥹❤️

Always remember, my baby: your efforts are not invisible, your patience is not forgotten, and your presence is not taken for granted. You will always be someone I appreciate, no matter what. ❤️❤️
